ArbitrEdge Need {{ mx.badges("synthetic", "demo") }}

The synthetic sportsbook accepts wagers from {{ "{:,}".format(s.health.synthetic_book.bettors) }} persistent simulated NFL customers. Each wager is accepted at a time-stamped price (here from demo quotes), and every ticket is kept in an append-only ledger. Need is the outcome that pays the book best. It is a liability fact about simulated customers, not a prediction and not a bet recommendation. Customer behaviour parameters are unvalidated assumptions.

This page still uses demo quotes. It switches to real collected odds once a few days of multi-book history exist.
The book closes to new wagers whenever quotes go stale (no fresh price within 45 minutes), so simulated customers never bet into prices that weren't posted.
