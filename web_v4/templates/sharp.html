Evidence of informed betting is read only from real price movement: which books moved first, steam across books, key-number crossings, buybacks, and market-making vs recreational disagreement. The 0–100 score is an uncalibrated heuristic. Simulated sportsbook customers are never used as evidence. In this build the quotes are demo data, so the signals demonstrate the method only.

Not available: reverse line movement and ticket-vs-money splits (no licensed betting-splits source), and injury news (no feed). The engine reports these as unavailable; it never estimates them.
